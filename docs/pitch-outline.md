# Pitch outline (8 slides)

1. **The problem.** Colleges hold seven systems that don't talk to each other. A student who is quietly slipping is invisible until results arrive. *(One real-feeling example: 7.9 CGPA, LMS activity down 60%, no mock interviews.)*
2. **The insight.** Early-warning systems that only flag students don't change outcomes. Purdue's widely copied Signals system was later found to have no proven causal effect on retention; Georgia State succeeded by tracking many specific markers and giving advisors the capacity to act. Ascora is built around the action after the flag.
3. **What Ascora does.** Joins 7 sources into one student view, scores success, finds two separate risks (academic, placement), explains every flag, and tracks the intervention.
4. **Integration (30%).** Messy files, fuzzy matching with confidence, review queue, conflicts with rules, lineage. 100% precision and 95% recall on fuzzy matches versus ground truth.
5. **Score and risk (25%).** Transparent weighted score, stable under weight shifts (rank correlation 0.997). Calibrated models with SHAP drivers. Segments with recommended actions. Intent-aware placement.
6. **Dashboard (25%).** Admin overview, Student 360, mentor weekly planner, what-if simulator, Ask Ascora.
7. **Trust.** Database-enforced roles, masked data, audit log, no student-facing labels, fairness audit with honest findings (four items flagged for review).
8. **Roadmap.** Mobile app for mentors, WhatsApp digest, live ERP/LMS connectors, multi-department rollout, accreditation reporting.

**Closing line:** "Most early-warning systems stop at the red flag. Ascora is built for what happens after it."
